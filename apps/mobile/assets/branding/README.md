## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished production app icon for SignBridge, a Korean sign language communication app. Square flat vector-like brand symbol: an elegant white open hand integrated with a curved bridge arch in its palm, representing sign language connecting people. A single small mint green accent at the bridge end. Bold simple rounded geometry, crisp silhouette, exceptionally legible at 48px. Solid deep royal blue #003D9B background filling the entire square edge to edge. Center symbol within central 60% safe area, generous blue margin for Android icon masking. No words, no letters, no mockup, no shadows, no gradients, no rounded outer tile corners. Deliver one square icon.
